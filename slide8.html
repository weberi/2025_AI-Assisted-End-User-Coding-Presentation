Impact on Teaching

Goal: Teach non-IT professionals exactly the IT and programming knowledge that will advance them most in their practice.

Questions for didactics and selection of teaching content:

  • What is the benefit of programming knowledge for non-IT professionals?
  • What types of IT knowledge are most useful today?
  • What motivates them to learn programming themselves?
  • What assessment formats are suitable?
